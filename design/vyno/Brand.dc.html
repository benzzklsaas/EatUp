The bubble motif

Organic, hand-drawn blob shapes — never perfect circles — layered at low opacity behind content, or bold as isolated accents. They stand in for the bubbles in a simmering pot, a fizzing drink, a full plate. Sizes and hues vary; the fluid silhouette never does.
